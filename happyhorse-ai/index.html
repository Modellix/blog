Summary

HappyHorse AI is Alibaba's open-source video model that debuted at #1 on the Artificial Analysis Video Arena. HappyHorse 1.1 now costs $0.14 to $0.18 per second. Here is how 1.1 compares with 1.0, what the benchmarks show, and how to try it.

HappyHorse AI is the open-source video model family from Alibaba’s Taotian Group. HappyHorse 1.0 appeared anonymously on the Artificial Analysis Video Arena in April 2026 and took #1 in both text-to-video and image-to-video within days. HappyHorse 1.1 is the current version, with the same native audio and a lower 1080p price.

On Modellix, HappyHorse 1.1 costs $0.14 per second at 720p and $0.18 at 1080p, with 3 to 15-second clips and native audio. It fits realistic people, product shots, and fast motion where you want audio in the same pass.

If you need a single 30-second take, or the top audio score on the arena, Seedance is still the stronger pick. The comparison below shows where each one wins.

How HappyHorse Generates Video

  • One Transformer for every modality. Text, image, video, and audio tokens run through a single 40-layer self-attention stack, so video and sound come out of the same forward pass instead of a separate audio model.
  • 15B parameters, 8 denoising steps. Distillation keeps inference short; the team reported about 38 seconds for a 1080p clip on one H100.
  • Native lip sync in seven languages. Mandarin, Cantonese, English, Japanese, Korean, German, and French.

These figures come from the team’s April release. The open weights let you verify them on your own hardware.

What Can You Build with HappyHorse

HappyHorse is at its best when a shot has to look filmed rather than generated.

  • Product and food ads. Close macro shots with liquid, steam, and texture, rendered with sound in one call.
  • UGC-style and lifestyle clips. Natural faces and hands for testimonial-style or social content.
  • Sports and action b-roll. Fast body motion with realistic motion blur.
  • Documentary and nature footage. Animals and landscapes that hold up at 1080p.

Where HappyHorse is the wrong choice

  • You need one continuous take longer than 15 seconds. Seedance 2.5 T2V and Wan 3.0 T2V reach 30 seconds.
  • Audio quality is the deciding factor. Seedance 2.0 leads the arena’s audio categories.
  • You need 4K output. HappyHorse stops at 1080p.

Third-party testers also flagged physics slips in the team’s own demos, such as a vehicle moving underwater with no bubbles. Run your hardest shot on both HappyHorse and Seedance before you commit.

Who Built HappyHorse

HappyHorse launched with no company name, no paper, and no launch post. The community spent two days testing theories, including links to Kuaishou and to the open-source daVinci-MagiHuman project.

On April 9, 2026, the team confirmed it is the Future Life Lab inside Alibaba’s Taotian Group, led by Zhang Di, who previously led the technical build of Kling 1.0 and 2.0 at Kuaishou. The weights, distilled versions, super-resolution module, and inference code were released the same day.

Third-party sites such as happyhorse.app and happyhorse.video use the name but have not confirmed they run this model. Treat them as separate products.

Frequently Asked Questions About HappyHorse AI

Who makes HappyHorse AI? The Future Life Lab in Alibaba’s Taotian Group, led by Zhang Di. The team confirmed its identity on April 9, 2026.

Is HappyHorse open source? Yes. The 1.0 weights, distilled versions, super-resolution module, and inference code are on GitHub under a commercial license. Check the license file before deployment.

How much does HappyHorse cost? On Modellix, HappyHorse 1.1 is $0.14 per second at 720p and $0.18 at 1080p. HappyHorse 1.0 is $0.14 and $0.24.

Should I use HappyHorse 1.1 or 1.0? Use 1.1 for new work: it is cheaper at 1080p and supports more aspect ratios. Keep 1.0 for the Video Edit route.

How does HappyHorse compare with Seedance 2.0? HappyHorse led Seedance 2.0 in the arena’s no-audio categories at launch; Seedance 2.0 led with audio. HappyHorse 1.1 is about half the price of Seedance 2.0 at 1080p.

Can I run HappyHorse locally? The team reports one NVIDIA H100 for about 38 seconds per 1080p clip. Consumer GPU versions were described as in development.
