The short answer: Hailuo 02 pricing is four different numbers

“Hailuo 02 pricing” has no single answer because the same model is sold in four billing units: subscription credits per month (the consumer Hailuo AI product), per completed video (MiniMax’s API and Replicate), per 10 seconds (AIMLAPI), and per second (fal.ai, kie.ai, Modellix). As of August 10, 2026, a 6-second 768P Hailuo 02 video costs about $0.27–$0.324 across the per-second and per-video routes, a 6-second 1080P video costs $0.29–$0.64, and no single route is cheapest on every cell — the winner changes by resolution, duration, and billing contract.

This article is from Modellix, an AI media-model API aggregator that resells Hailuo 02 on per-second pricing, so we have a commercial interest in this comparison. Every figure below was read from the listed source on August 10, 2026, and where sources conflict we say so instead of picking a side.

The practical rule: normalize every quote to per-second and per-clip before comparing, then pick a route by your volume and workflow — that is what the tables and worked examples below do.

Why “Hailuo 02 pricing” has no single answer: four billing units

MiniMax sells the same underlying model through two product lines, and aggregators resell it under their own contracts. Each contract publishes the price in a different unit, which makes naive comparison worse than useless — it is actively misleading.

Billing unit Who bills this way Example quote (as of Aug 10, 2026)
Subscription credits / month MiniMax consumer product (hailuoai.video) $14.99–$199.99 per month for 1,000–20,000 credits
Per completed video MiniMax developer API, Replicate $0.28 for 768P 6s (MiniMax); $0.27 for 768P 6s (Replicate)
Per 10 seconds AIMLAPI $0.728 per 10-second output
Per second fal.ai, kie.ai, Modellix $0.045/s (fal, 768P); $0.0475/s (kie, 1080P); $0.0540/s (Modellix, 768P 6s)

Two warnings before you use any of these numbers:

  1. Sources disagree, and the freshest one is not necessarily the official one. MiniMax’s own payment-policy document (last updated July 14, 2025) lists the Standard subscription at $14.99/month for 1,000 credits, while the ImagineArt Hailuo pricing guide (updated March 25, 2026) lists it at $9.99/month for the same credits. We are not going to resolve that conflict for you — check the live pricing UI in the app before you commit. The same discipline applies to every cell in this article: each price carries the date it was read.
  2. “Hailuo 02 pricing” also surfaces prices for other models. Several ranking pages quote Hailuo 2.3 or even Video-01 while ranking for “hailuo 02.” Always confirm the model name, resolution, and duration in the same sentence as the price.

What moves the price: resolution, duration, and mode

Within a route, the number you pay per clip is the product of three knobs. Lock these down before you estimate anything, because the same model can span a 6× price range.

Knob Options (Hailuo 02) Effect on price
Resolution 512P / 768P / 1080P Biggest lever. On Modellix I2V, 512P is $0.0200/s vs $0.1060/s at 1080P 6s — more than 5×
Duration 6s / 10s Linear on per-second routes; per-video routes charge fixed tiers (e.g. $0.28 vs $0.56 at 768P on MiniMax direct)
Mode T2V / I2V / FL2V Same base rate on MiniMax direct; Modellix publishes I2V from $0.0200/s (512P) and T2V/FL2V from $0.0540/s

MiniMax’s prepaid Video Packages make the resolution/duration math explicit in video points: one 768P 6s Hailuo 02 video deducts 1 point, a 768P 10s deducts 2, a 1080P 6s deducts 2, and 512P clips deduct 0.3–0.5. At the Standard pack ($1,000 for 3,760 points, one-month validity), that works out to roughly $0.27 per 768P 6s clip and $0.53 per 1080P 6s clip — comparable to the per-video list prices, before you account for the $1,000 minimum and the expiry clock. The official Video Packages page is the source for both the pack table and the deduction table; note it also says MiniMax H3 is not supported by these packages yet.

The same knob logic applies to the Modellix I2V route: the Hailuo 02 I2V model page publishes a per-dimension table (512P/768P/1080P × 6s/10s) so the exact cell you intend to run is the exact cell you are quoted.

Direct MiniMax or an aggregator: how to choose

Use a route table like the one above as the input, then apply a decision rule that has nothing to do with price:

  • Pick MiniMax direct when Hailuo is your only video model, you already run MiniMax’s platform for other models, and you are comfortable with per-video billing and prepaid packs. Direct is also where new model announcements land first — Hailuo 02 is currently labeled a legacy model there, with MiniMax H3 as the current headline, so check the model table if you are starting fresh.
  • Pick an aggregator when your product spans several video and image models. The cost that matters is not per-second price but per-vendor workflow: accounts, keys, response schemas, and polling logic multiply with every vendor. An aggregator like Modellix trades that surface for one key and one billing dashboard, with per-second pricing and per-call cost logs across Hailuo, Kling, Wan, Seedance, and the rest. Our MiniMax provider page lists the current routes, and the MiniMax free API guide separates documented free endpoints from paid video access.
  • Watch the traps either way: pack validity (one month), per-route duration caps (fal’s Standard stops at 6s), resolution availability at 10s (1080P 10s is not available on every route), and the standing source conflicts we flagged above. Re-read the live price cell for your exact combination before you commit — every number in this article is dated August 10, 2026, and pricing moves.

The honest boundary: if you need one price sheet for one model, MiniMax direct is the simplest source of truth. If you are comparing routes because your product needs several models, the aggregator’s value is the workflow axis, not a universal price floor — on the 1080P 6s cell above, kie.ai’s list price is the lowest, and Modellix does not dispute that.

FAQ

How much does Hailuo AI cost?

The consumer subscription product lists plans from about $14.99/month for 1,000 credits up to $199.99/month for 20,000 credits in MiniMax’s payment-policy document (July 14, 2025). A fresher third-party guide lists the entry plan lower; the official live pricing UI is the arbiter. On the API side, Hailuo 02 is billed per video or per second depending on route (see the normalized table above).

How much does one Hailuo 02 video cost?

As of August 10, 2026: $0.28 for 768P 6s and $0.49 for 1080P 6s on MiniMax direct (per video); $0.27 and $0.48 on fal.ai and Replicate; $0.324 and $0.636 on Modellix; about $0.285 for 1080P 6s on kie.ai. Normalize the unit, then compare.

Is Hailuo 02 pricing per second or per video?

Both, depending on the route. MiniMax direct and Replicate bill per completed video; AIMLAPI bills per 10 seconds; fal.ai, kie.ai, and Modellix bill per second. The billing unit is part of the route contract — match it before comparing.

Is Hailuo AI free?

There is no documented permanent free API tier for Hailuo 02. The consumer product offers free credits for new users (the official site advertises signup credits), and some aggregators pair new accounts with starter credit — treat any credit as a test budget, not a production cost baseline. MiniMax’s official Pay as You Go page is the reference for what is actually billed.

Is Hailuo 02 deprecated?

MiniMax’s model documentation labels Hailuo 02 a legacy video model — stable, documented, and still priced and callable, but no longer the marketing headline (MiniMax H3 is). “Legacy” does not mean shut down; verify the live model table before planning around it.

What is the difference between Hailuo 02 and Hailuo 2.3 pricing?

On MiniMax direct they are priced identically ($0.28 / $0.56 / $0.49 for 768P 6s / 768P 10s / 1080P 6s), and there is a cheaper Hailuo 2.3 Fast tier ($0.19–$0.33 per video). Hailuo 2.3 is retuned for human motion and stylized art; Hailuo 02 is the physics-and-motion pick with 1080P 10s support and a first/last-frame workflow.

How much does MiniMax AI cost?

It depends on the product line: media generation (Hailuo 02, Hailuo 2.3, image models) is billed per video, per second, or per image; MiniMax’s LLMs (MiniMax-M3) are billed per million tokens. The official Pay as You Go page carries both, and our MiniMax pricing breakdown explains the package side.

Which is cheaper: the Hailuo AI subscription or the API?

For consumer-scale use, the subscription’s monthly credit balance is simpler and includes the app experience. For production volume, per-second and per-video API routes are typically an order of magnitude cheaper per clip (roughly $0.27–$0.64 per 6-second clip vs the subscription’s third-party-estimated ~$2.50 per clip). They are different products priced for different buyers — compare per clip, per month, at your actual volume.
